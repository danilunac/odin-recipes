Bikini Martini

Photo by Dextar Vision on Unsplash

Description

An awesome drink to sip by the pool! Garnish with an orange wheel.

Ingredients

  • 1 fluid ounce coconut rum
  • ¾ fluid ounce vodka
  • 1 fluid ounce pineapple juice
  • 1 dash grenadine syrup

Steps

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice. Add rum, vodka, and pineapple juice
  2. Shake firmly until frothy and well chilled
  3. Strain into a chilled martini glass
  4. Add a touch of grenadine in the center. Garnish with an orange wheel
